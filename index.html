Process

Designed for momentum without chaos.

The operating rhythm is intentionally simple: scope narrowly, build visibly, keep records, and turn every release into a clearer next step.

01

Map the intent

Define the user, the product promise, the launch constraint, and the thing that must not break.

02

Prototype the system

Build the first usable version with realistic data, mobile-ready UI, and a path to public deployment.

03

Prepare the surface

Set up domain, support email, product pages, privacy posture, and distribution checklist.

04

Ship, observe, refine

Launch small, learn from behavior, remove noise, and compound what actually works.
